# Changelog

## v1
- First release
- Zygisk engine: spoofs the device only inside the games you pick
- Script engine as a fallback for setups without Zygisk
- WebUI: master switch, device picker with phone icons, searchable game list, auto-save
- Presets: ROG Phone 9 Pro, OnePlus 15, Galaxy S26 Ultra and S25 Ultra, Pixel 10 Pro and 9 Pro, Red Magic 10 Pro and more, plus a Custom device
- Update support: new releases show as updates in Magisk / KernelSU managers
